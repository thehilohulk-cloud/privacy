## 1. SHOOT LOGISTICS — ONE HALF-DAY (~4.5 hrs)
7:30 setup (tripod, lav check, teleprompter, test clip w/ headphones) · 8:00–9:45 Block A "Consult corner" (Outfit A): scripts 8, 1, 6, 4, 10 · 9:45 break + OUTFIT CHANGE · 10:00–11:30 Block B training floor (Outfit B): scripts 2, 3, 5, 9, 7 · 11:30–12:15 b-roll (Titus closeups → scan station → floor) · 12:15 BACKUP everything to laptop + Drive 01-raw before leaving.
Rules: Script 8 films FIRST (show-rate video, freshest energy). Two takes max per script. Group by outfit/location, never re-light mid-block. Camera = newest phone, horizontal 4K/30, tripod at EYE level, locked exposure/focus. Audio = lav + always-running voice-memo backup phone (clap to sync). Teleprompter app on tablet under the lens. Window light first; one soft LED 45° if gray. Kill the playlist/AC/ice machine; record 10s room tone. Slate every take ("Eight, take one").
B-roll checklists (no member faces — consent): (1) training floor ×8 (coaching stance, racking DBs, chalk hands, bench clicks, walk-through, whiteboard, feet setting stance, arms-folded "20 years" wide); (2) FitIndex station ×6 (wide, feet stepping on barefoot, app syncing w/ GARRETT'S data, over-shoulder point, 12-point closeup, from-the-consult-chair POV); (3) Titus phone closeups ×7 on a DEMO thread (contact card w/ 515-8086, typing+send, reply w/ macros, THE MONEY SHOT: menu photo → Titus order reply [rehearse once], week-of-check-ins scroll, face lit by phone, phone on table).

## 2. SCRIPTS — 10 OBJECTION PAIRS (full verbatim text in Drive copy)
Each: title-card hook = the objection verbatim (first 2s) → 60–90s camera-direct script w/ inline b-roll cues → soft CTA matched to home surface → chains to the next objection.

3. "I'm too out of shape to start" — cleaning the house before the housekeeper; the program scaled to day one; come in slippers. → 4
4. "It's too expensive" — door key vs coach; add up the last five years; cheap-that-doesn't-work is the expensive thing. → 5
5. "I don't want to give up my foods" — KEEP YOUR RICE; the Titus menu-photo demo (the differentiator video). → 9

7. "I can't stay consistent / I always quit" — nobody quits on purpose; the quiet drift; Titus check-ins + the we-notice rescue text. → 8
8. **"What actually happens at the consult?" (THE SHOW-RATE VIDEO — films first)** — the whole 30 minutes demystified: door, scan (60s, slippers), talk, no workout, no pressure, no obligation.
9. "An AI coach? That sounds weird" — Titus = a phone number, no app; never forgets your numbers; covers the other 165 hours; not replacing Garrett. → 7
10. "I fell off after my last program" (ALUMNI ONLY) — retire the phrase; seasons change; muscle memory is physiology; your numbers are still in our system; the door never closed. CTA: text BACK / book the scan.

## 4. METRICS — DAY-30 COLD-UNLOCK GATE
**UNLOCK IF: show-rate lift ≥10 pts (video-receiving vs baseline/non-receiving) OR ≥5 attributable bookings in 30 days.**
Measure: (1) 90-day baseline show rate pulled BEFORE first send (Kim); cohorts = received ≥1 video vs 0. (2) Attributable bookings via UTM click OR the consult question "Did you watch any of the short videos we sent — which ones?" logged to GHL `video_seen` multi-select (Garrett asks, Della logs); no double-dipping wins between metrics. (3) Per-video watch signals weekly (YouTube Studio avg % viewed — under ~40% = rewrite send copy first, re-shoot second; UTM clicks per surface; Titus send-vs-click counts). (4) Garrett-hours + capacity check (the council's blind spot — if consult volume strains the calendar, address before any cold spend).
Day-30 review (Garrett + Kim, 30 min): lift ≥10? bookings ≥5? either yes → scope cold V2 (SEO/thumbnails/Shorts/comment-routing, in that order). Both no → watched-but-no-lift = content problem (revise weakest scripts); not-watched = distribution problem (fix sends, extend 30d). Also verify: alumni-answer theme audit + Social Planner OAuth done (5/5 platforms connected).
