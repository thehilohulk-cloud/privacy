Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Introduction

Transformations Personal Training Studio ("we," "our," or "us") operates the website at transformations.studio, related program pages, and the TPTS Ads API application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

This policy covers our website and messaging tools as well as our advertising operations. It applies whether you visit a page, start a chat with us, book a consultation, or interact with our ads.

Information We Collect

Information you provide to us

We collect information you give us directly, including when you start a chat on our website, submit a form, or book a consultation:

  • Name, phone number, and email address
  • The content of messages you send us through our website chat, and our replies
  • Appointment details, including the times you book and whether you attend
  • Goals, interests, or other details you choose to share about what you are looking for

Our website chat is assisted by an automated system that helps answer questions and schedule consultations. Conversations may be reviewed by our staff. Please do not send us health, medical, or other sensitive information through the chat.

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, we and our service providers automatically collect:

  • IP address and general location derived from it, such as city or region
  • Device and browser type, operating system, and screen size
  • Pages viewed, links clicked, time spent, and the site or ad that referred you
  • Cookies and similar identifiers, as described under Cookies and Tracking Technologies

If you reach our website from an email or text message we sent you, we may associate that visit with your existing contact record so we can follow up appropriately.

Advertising data

Our application integrates with Meta's Marketing API to manage advertising campaigns for Transformations Personal Training Studio. We collect and process:

  • Ad account performance data (impressions, clicks, conversions)
  • Campaign management data (ad creative assets, targeting parameters)
  • Business page interaction data necessary for ad delivery

If you message us through Facebook or Instagram, we receive the contents of those messages and the profile information those platforms make available to us.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your questions and schedule consultations
  • Contact you about a consultation you requested, including reminders
  • Send you marketing messages about our programs, where you have agreed to receive them
  • Provide, personalize, and improve our coaching services
  • Measure and improve our website and our advertising
  • Maintain the security of our systems and meet our legal obligations

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies, including advertising pixels provided by Meta, to operate our website, understand how it is used, and measure the performance of our advertising. These tools may allow us or our advertising partners to show you ads for our programs after you leave our site.

Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies through their settings. You can also limit ad personalization through the settings offered by the relevant advertising platform. Refusing cookies may affect how parts of our website work.

We do not respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals at this time.

Text Messaging

If you give us your phone number and agree to be contacted by text, we may send you messages about your consultation, your program, and our offers. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. You may still receive messages necessary to confirm or change an appointment you have booked. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us using the details below.

We do not sell or share your phone number with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We do share information with service providers who process it on our behalf, and only as needed to run our business. These include:

  • GoHighLevel — our customer relationship management, scheduling, and messaging platform, which stores your contact record, appointments, and chat history
  • Meta Platforms — for advertising delivery and measurement, and for messages you send us through Facebook or Instagram
  • Analytics, email, and text messaging providers that support our website and communications

These providers are permitted to use your information only to provide services to us. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or in connection with a sale or transfer of our business.

Data Retention

We retain contact records, appointment history, and chat transcripts for as long as needed to provide our services and maintain our business relationship with you, and afterward as required by law or to resolve disputes. We retain advertising performance data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy. You may request deletion as described below.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect the information we process against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, and to ask us to stop sending you marketing communications. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below and we will respond as required by applicable law.

You can also:

  • Reply STOP to any text message to stop marketing texts
  • Use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email
  • Adjust cookie settings in your browser

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your state or country's privacy laws, including the right to know what personal information we have collected and the right to request its deletion. We do not discriminate against anyone for exercising these rights.

Children's Privacy

Our programs and our website are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Third-Party Links

Our website may link to sites we do not operate, including scheduling tools and partner products. This policy does not apply to those sites, and we encourage you to read their privacy policies.
